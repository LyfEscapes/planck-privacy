Planck

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 7, 2026

Planck is a game by LyfEscapes LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Planck on Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Planck has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no servers. We never see your name, email, contacts, photos, files or location.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, quanta, settings and which items you own are stored only on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your own Google account backup so it can be restored to a new phone; we cannot see that backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Planck shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad to re-cohere after losing or to double your quanta, and a full-screen ad after every third run. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about your device, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before any ad is shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the Store screen ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads).

If you buy Planck+, the app no longer shows ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

Planck is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Data sent by the Google services above travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
